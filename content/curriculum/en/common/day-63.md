Two days to launch. If your stomach is doing something odd, good — this matters to you. Fear left vague grows;
fear written down shrinks to its real size.

## 1. Name it (15 min)

Three lines, in this order:

1. **The fear, in one sentence.** Be blunt. "Nobody will care." "Someone will find a bug in front of everyone."
   "People I know will see it and think it's cringe."
2. **The worst *realistic* outcome.** Not the movie version. Usually: "12 people visit, 2 sign up, one says the
   button is ugly." Or: "the video gets 80 views and one rude comment."
3. **How you'd repair it.** "Fix the bug, post a short update." "Delete the rude comment, keep posting weekly."

Look at line 3. You've been doing repairs like that for nine weeks.

## The small surprise: a note to launch-morning you

Write three lines to the person who wakes up on day 65 and schedule them to arrive that morning — a scheduled email,
a phone reminder, or a scheduled message to yourself in your messenger. Something like:

```text
You're launching something you built from nothing in 65 days.
Ship it before 11:00, then reply to every comment — that's the whole job.
Whatever the numbers say, day 1 you would be stunned.
```

## 2. The 10-minute pre-check (10 min)

Set a timer. No fixing during it — just note what's broken; fixes go to tomorrow's polish day.

- **Apps:** open the live link in a private window on your phone; sign up with a fresh email; reach the main
  value in under two minutes; trigger one error on purpose and confirm it shows up in your error log; check that
  analytics records the visit; confirm payments are in the mode you intend.
- **Video:** play the final master on your phone with sound, then muted; captions in sync to the end; loudness
  checked; thumbnail readable at the size of a fingernail; title, description and links correct; publish time set
  in the right time zone.

> **If you're stuck:** if the list turns up more than five problems, don't panic — pick the three that block a
> stranger from getting value or finishing the video. The rest can wait until after launch.

## You're done when…

- [ ] You pasted your fear, the worst realistic outcome and your repair.
- [ ] You pasted your pre-check results: what passed, what goes into tomorrow's fixes.

Then close the laptop. Rest is part of the launch plan.
