# FlowLeaf 66 — program outline (the skeleton)

This is the **binding skeleton** for both languages. Day numbers, tracks, task ids, task types, XP and proof types
below are the contract: `ru` and `en` day files must match it exactly. Titles here are English working titles; each
language writes its own native title, summary and body (see `WRITING-GUIDE.md`). Pedagogy lives in `DESIGN.md`.

Legend: task = `id · type · xp · proof — what the student does`. Flags: 🛌 rest day, 🐉 boss day, 🏅 badge(s) awarded.

## Phases

| # | Days | Name | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–14 | Foundation | Ship on day 2, learn to direct an agent, call APIs and AI models, deploy a backend, pick a track, solve one real person's problem. |
| 2 | 15–35 | Build | Core craft of the track: full-stack AI apps and bots / code-made video from frames to a one-command pipeline. Boss: a real product or a published explainer. |
| 3 | 36–52 | Ship | Real users and real clients: launch pages, feedback loops, parallel agents, MCP, background jobs / publishing, series, pipelines as apps, dubbing. Capstone starts. |
| 4 | 53–66 | Portfolio & Earn | Finish and launch the capstone, build the portfolio, find and pitch paid work, graduate with a plan for the next 66 days. |

## Shape at a glance

- **Common days (both tracks):** 1–14, every rest day (21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56, 63), the earning block 57–60, and 66.
- **Track days (apps + video, separate files):** 15–20, 22–27, 29–34, 36–41, 43–48, 50–55, 61, 62, 64, 65.
- **Boss days:** 13 (common), 27, 41, 55, 65 (per track).
- Typical day: 75–120 min, 90–140 XP. Rest day: 30–40 min, 35–45 XP. Boss day: 150–180 min, 200–240 XP.
- XP by task type (guideline): read/watch 10 · quiz 15 · reflect 15–20 · share 20–30 · build 20–80 · ship 50–120.
