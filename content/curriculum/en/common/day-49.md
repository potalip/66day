Seven weeks. Tomorrow you choose your capstone — the thing you'll launch publicly on day 65. Big choices made on an
empty tank turn into big regrets, so today you refuel first and dream second.

## Today's ritual: a walk with one question

Ten minutes outside. No podcast, no music, phone in airplane mode. Take exactly one question with you:

> "Which hour of the last seven weeks did I lose track of time?"

Don't try to answer it. Just walk. The answer usually shows up around minute seven, uninvited.

## 1. Energy check (10 min)

Back at your desk, make two short lists from the last two weeks:

- **Drains:** tasks that left you flat. Maybe it was fighting OAuth redirects, chasing a render that kept
  desyncing, or writing metadata for ten uploads.
- **Fuels:** moments you'd happily repeat. Watching a stranger use your app. The first time your pipeline turned a
  topic into a finished short without you touching it. A client saying "oh, that's exactly it".

Then one line: *what will I protect in the final 17 days?* (A morning slot, a no-phone hour, sleep.)

## 2. The capstone shortlist (10 min)

Write three ideas, one line each. Run every idea through three filters:

1. **Would you use or watch it yourself** — or pay for it?
2. **Can a v1 go live in about 15 days**, at your usual 1–2 hours a day?
3. **Does it show off what you learned** — and point at the kind of paid work you want?

Examples to spark ideas, not to copy:

- Apps: a Telegram booking assistant for a local studio, with payments; an internal dashboard for a friend's shop
  that answers "what sold this week?"; a voice tool that turns meeting notes into tasks.
- Video: a weekly 60-second explainer series about your city; a tool that cuts a long podcast into three shorts;
  a client-grade promo reel for a real small business.

Circle the idea your "fuels" list points to. That's usually the one.

> **Tip:** if all three ideas feel too big, cut each one in half and check again. Small and live is better than
> ambitious and stuck on day 64.

## You're done when…

- [ ] You pasted your drains, your fuels and the one thing you'll protect.
- [ ] You pasted three capstone ideas, one line each, with your favourite circled.

Tomorrow: pick one and write its spec.
