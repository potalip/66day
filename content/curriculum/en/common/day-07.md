A week ago you hadn't installed an agent. Since then you've shipped a live page, tamed the terminal, written a real
spec, read your own code and time-travelled with git. Today is light on purpose: rest is part of the habit, not a
break from it. Close the laptop when you're done.

## Look back (15 min)

Write five sentences, one for each prompt. Honest beats polished.

1. What **surprised** you this week?
2. What was **hard** — the moment you almost gave up?
3. What are you **proud** of?
4. What will you do differently next week (time of day, length of sessions, how you prompt)?
5. One thing you'd tell someone starting day 1 tomorrow.

Keep it somewhere you'll find on day 66. Future you will laugh at how scary `cd ..` used to be.

## Tell the world (15 min)

Building in public is a cheat code: it makes you finish things, and it slowly attracts people who need what you
make. Start small. Post on X, Threads, LinkedIn, Bluesky — wherever your people already are.

A template to make your own:

```text
Week 1 of a 66-day challenge: learning to build with AI coding agents.

I shipped my first web page (with a working day counter): https://mira-builds.github.io/66-day-promise/

Lesson of the week: a vague prompt gets you a guess; a clear spec gets you what you wanted.

Day 7 of 66. See you next week.
```

Add a screenshot — posts with an image get noticed more. If posting publicly feels like too much, a post in a group
chat or a community you belong to counts too, as long as you can link to it.

> **Tip:** one reply from a stranger asking "how did you make that?" is worth more than fifty likes. Answer it —
> that's your first student, or your first client.
