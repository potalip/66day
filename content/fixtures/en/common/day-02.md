## Vague in, vague out

"Make it better" gives the agent nothing to aim at. Good prompts have four parts:

1. **Goal** — what the thing is for. *"A page I can send to a recruiter."*
2. **Context** — what exists already. *"index.html from yesterday."*
3. **Constraints** — what must stay true. *"No frameworks, works on a phone, loads fast."*
4. **Done when** — how you'll judge it. *"Name is the first thing you see; one clear button to email me."*

## Practice

Make three changes to your page, each with a four-part prompt. For example:

```
Goal: make the page feel personal.
Context: index.html in this folder.
Constraints: keep it one file, keep the calm colors.
Done when: there is a short "Why I'm doing this" section with 3 bullet points I can edit.
```

After each change, look at the page on your phone (ask the agent how). Small screens reveal everything.

> **Note:** If the agent goes off in the wrong direction, don't argue for ten messages. Say *"undo that"* and write a clearer prompt.

## You're done when…

- your page has three improvements you asked for on purpose;
- you've pasted your best prompt below;
- you've rewritten this one into a four-part prompt: *"make a site for my cafe"*.
