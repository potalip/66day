Launch day is in three days. On day 55 you premiered quietly; on day 65 you go loud. A big launch needs one thing a
premiere didn't: a **trailer** — 30 seconds that make a stranger want the whole thing. Movie studios spend months on
trailers. You have a pipeline, a transcript of everything you've made, and an agent. That's enough.

## A trailer is a promise, not a summary

A summary says "here's what happens". A trailer says "here's what you'll *feel* — and you'll only get it if you
watch". The shape that works for almost everything:

1. **0–3 s — The hook.** The single most surprising line or image you have.
2. **3–20 s — The world.** 3–5 quick beats that show range: different episodes, different outputs, the "how is this
   made by code?" moments.
3. **20–27 s — The turn.** One line of what it is and who it's for.
4. **27–30 s — The ask.** Name, date, where to watch. ("New episode every Tuesday." "Try it free at…")

For a **tool** capstone, the trailer shows input → output, fast, three times. For a **showreel**, the reel *is* the
long version — the trailer is its best 30 seconds plus your name and contact.

## Step by step

### 1. Let the pipeline cut the trailer (45 min)

You already built every piece: auto-clipping from transcripts (day 46), edit decision lists (day 30), brand components
(day 32), multi-format export (day 34). Now chain them:

```text
Goal: a 30-second trailer for my capstone, generated by the pipeline as trailer.edl.json + rendered mp4.
Context: read the transcripts and storyboard JSON for all capstone outputs in out/, and my brand config.
Steps:
1. Propose 8 candidate moments (source file, in/out timecodes, the line spoken, why it's strong) ranked for a
   trailer: surprise, visual variety, emotion. Wait for me to pick 4–5.
2. Build trailer.edl.json: hook (0–3 s) → 3–5 beats (3–20 s) → one-line "what it is" card (20–27 s) →
   end card with name + "where/when" (27–30 s). Cuts on music beats if a music track is set.
3. Render 16:9 (1920x1080) and 9:16 (1080x1920) versions with captions in the safe zone.
4. Run qc.py on both.
Done when: both mp4s are in out/trailer/, total length is 28–32 s, QC is clean, and the EDL is committed.
```

Watch it three times: normally, muted, and on your phone at arm's length. If the hook doesn't make *you* lean in, swap
it. Ask one person: "After this, what do you think the series/tool is?" If they can't answer, the 20–27 s card needs
plainer words.

> **If you're stuck:** the auto-picked moments feel random? Override the selection by hand — write the 5 timecodes
> yourself into the EDL. The pipeline still does the boring parts (cuts, captions, formats, loudness); you keep the
> taste.

**Proof for task 1:** attach the 16:9 trailer mp4.

### 2. The channel plan (25 min)

One **main** channel and two or three **supporting** ones. Read each community's rules today, not on launch morning.

| Channel | Format | Rules to respect |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | full episode / demo + trailer as a Short | Upload captions (SRT) and chapters; a pinned comment with a question. |
| Shorts / Reels / TikTok | the 9:16 trailer + 1–2 more cuts | Upload natively to each; avoid other platforms' watermarks. |
| X / Threads / LinkedIn | a thread or post with the trailer attached | Video uploaded directly, not just a link. |
| Reddit | a niche community where your viewers are | Each subreddit has self-promotion rules; many require regular participation first. Read the sidebar; ask the mods if unsure. |
| Show HN / Product Hunt | only if your capstone is a **tool** people can try | Show HN titles start with "Show HN:" and people must be able to try it. Never ask for upvotes. |

For each channel write: **date and local time**, **which file**, **which title/caption** (from your metadata script),
and **who you'll personally tell** — your day 54 test viewers, the day 41 client, creators you've talked to.
**Proof for task 2:** paste the plan.

### 3. Pre-launch checklist (20 min)

Package and schedule everything now, so launch day is pressing "post" and talking to people. YouTube can publish at a
set time if you upload as private with a `publishAt` timestamp:

```bash
python upload.py out/episode-03.mp4 --meta out/episode-03.json --privacy private --publish-at 2026-11-07T15:00:00Z
```

Ask your agent to add `--publish-at` to the upload script if it doesn't have it (check the current YouTube Data API
docs for the `status.publishAt` field). Then mark each item:

- [ ] Every launch video: final title, description with chapters, tags, language, category set.
- [ ] Thumbnails chosen and checked at phone size; captions (SRT) uploaded, not just burned in.
- [ ] AI-content disclosure set where the platform asks for it; music and font licences on file.
- [ ] Playlist for the series; end screens or a final card pointing to the next video.
- [ ] Scheduled uploads verified in the studio (right time zone!).
- [ ] Links in descriptions work, and your service page from day 61 is linked.
