## Why rest is part of the program

Habits are built by showing up, not by burning out. Rest days still count for your streak —
they're short on purpose, so the chain doesn't break on a tired day.

## Look back (10 minutes)

Open everything you made so far: your page, your boss project. Then answer honestly:

- **What worked?** A prompt, a trick, a moment where it clicked.
- **What didn't?** Where did you get stuck, and how long did it take to get unstuck?
- **What's next?** One thing you want to be able to do by the end of the week.

> **Tip:** Put tomorrow's hour in your calendar right now. The best predictor of tomorrow's session is a time slot that already exists.

## You're done when…

- you wrote your three sentences below;
- tomorrow's hour is in your calendar.
